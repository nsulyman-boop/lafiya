    ✓Trust

    How we keep the information honest

    A wrong number here costs more than a wrong number anywhere else. These are the rules.

    Hospitals update their own status

    Only verified hospital staff can change doctor, oxygen and blood numbers. Every card shows when it was last updated, and anything older than 30 minutes is flagged.

    Guides follow standard protocols

    Emergency steps are written from internationally recognised first-aid guidance. Translations are reviewed by native speakers before they leave the English-only state.

    Nothing about you is stored

    No account, no sign-in. Your symptom summary lives only on your phone until you choose to copy or show it.

    In an emergency, call 112.

    Free from any network in Nigeria. In Lagos you can also call 767.
